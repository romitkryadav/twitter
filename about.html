How to use the tools

Open the tool that matches your link, paste a public post or profile URL, and follow the on-screen steps. Video and image tools work with supported public posts; profile-picture and follower tools use public profile information. You do not need to enter your X password to use the tools.

What appears can depend on the link, the post’s visibility, and whether X makes the requested information available at that time. Private or protected content is not made accessible by this site, and deleted, restricted, or temporarily unavailable posts may not load.

What to expect from follower counts

The follower counter checks the available profile data periodically while the page is visible and tracking is active. A refresh does not guarantee a new number: X may return the same count or delay reflecting a recent change. The graph shows observations from the current visit, not a long-term history or a prediction.

Use media responsibly

Only access and save content you are allowed to use. Downloading a copy does not transfer ownership or grant permission to repost, publish, or reuse it. Respect creators’ rights, platform terms, and applicable laws.

Independent service

Twitter Downloader is an independent third-party tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by X Corp. or Twitter. “X” and “Twitter” are used only to identify the platform the tools are designed to work with.
