Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you use Twitter Downloader, a set of tools for publicly available X (formerly Twitter) media and profile information. It describes the current website code and the services it calls. The site does not ask you to create an account or provide your X password.

Information you submit

When you use a lookup, the username or public post/profile URL you enter is sent to a processing service so it can request the relevant public information. Depending on the tool, the request is sent to a Cloudflare Worker endpoint for video, images, profile pictures, or follower counts. A download request may also include the public media URL and a suggested filename so the service can return the file.

These requests can reveal the profile name, post identifier, or other information contained in the URL. Do not submit private information or links you are not authorized to use. The site has no account database or saved lookup-history feature in its front-end code.

Service providers and request logs

The processing endpoints run on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare and the Worker service may process the submitted request and technical connection information to deliver, secure, and operate the lookup. The Worker backend is separate from the website files, so this site does not specify how long its operational logs are kept. For more information about Cloudflare’s handling of information, see its Privacy Policy.

Analytics, cookies, and browser storage

Google Analytics is loaded on the dedicated video, image, and profile-picture downloader pages. The configured measurement ID is G-SP0ZHQ7HRQ. Google Analytics may use first-party identifiers such as the _ga cookie and collect site-usage information, session statistics, browser/device details, and approximate location for measurement. The homepage, follower-counter page, and these policy pages do not load that Google Analytics tag in the current website code. Read Google’s Analytics data-collection overview and Google’s Privacy Policy.

The image and profile-picture tools save your selected color theme in your browser’s local storage. This preference stays on your device unless you clear site data. The current pages do not include an on-site analytics cookie preference control; you can manage or clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings.

Information from third-party sources

To display results, the tools may request public post or profile data from X-related services and load images or fonts from external hosts. Those providers receive the technical information associated with requests to their services and handle it under their own privacy policies. Twitter Downloader does not control the privacy practices of those providers.

How information is used

  • To look up public media or profile information requested through the tools.
  • To return previews or downloads that the selected tool supports.
  • To measure site usage on pages where Google Analytics is installed.
  • To help operate and protect the site and its processing services.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose not to use a lookup, avoid submitting a URL, and use your browser settings to block or clear cookies and local storage. Depending on where you live, applicable privacy laws may give you rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. For a privacy question or request, contact contact.themistero@gmail.com. We may need enough information to understand and respond to your request.

Children’s privacy

The tools are general-purpose utilities and are not designed to collect information from children. Do not submit personal information about a child through the site.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the site or its service providers change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when this version was prepared.
