How to download a video from Twitter

Copy a public post link, choose a video quality, and save the MP4.

Copy the post link

Open the post with the video, choose Share, and copy its link. Use the post URL, not the account profile link.

Paste the link and find the video

Paste the URL into the box above and select Download. If the post is public and has a supported video, the available qualities appear below.

Preview a quality and save

Select a resolution to preview it, then use its download button. Your browser saves the MP4 to its configured download location.

Download a video from Twitter or X

Use this Twitter video downloader to save a video attached to a public post. Paste the post URL into the box above; the page checks for available video qualities and lets you preview a version before downloading it. Public posts do not require you to sign in to X.

Copy the link to the specific post

Use the URL for the post that contains the video. It usually includes /status/, such as x.com/username/status/123456789; older twitter.com links work too. For a video in a quoted post, copy the link to the post that actually contains the video.

Choose a video quality

Available resolutions depend on the source video and what can be retrieved from the post. Choose one of the listed options to preview it, then download that version as an MP4. The tool does not upscale or convert the video.

Download on a phone or computer

Use the page in a modern browser on a phone or computer. Find the saved file in the browser’s downloads list or your device’s Downloads location. On iPhone, check Files under Downloads; to move a video to Photos, open it in Files and use Share if that option is available.

Why a Twitter video may not download

  • Wrong link: Use the specific post URL, which includes /status/, rather than a profile, search, or timeline page.
  • No video attachment: A post may contain a photo, plain text, or a link preview to a video hosted elsewhere. This page downloads videos attached to the X post.
  • Private or unavailable post: Protected posts, deleted posts, and content that requires sign-in are not accessible here.
  • Temporary source issue: If the post is public and contains a video but no quality loads, the source may be temporarily unavailable. Check the link and try again later.

The tool does not bypass account privacy settings. Downloading a video also does not grant permission to repost or reuse it; ask the creator when permission is required.
